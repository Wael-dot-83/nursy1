### Welcome to the Nursery Management System

This guide will help parents effectively use our childcare management platform to stay connected with their children's daily activities, attendance, and development.

## Getting Started

### Account Setup

1. **Receive Invitation**
   - You'll receive an SMS with your phone number and temporary password
   - Or contact your nursery administrator for account creation

2. **First Login**
   - Visit the application website
   - Enter your phone number
   - Enter the OTP code sent to your phone
   - Change your password when prompted

3. **Complete Profile**
   - Add your full name
   - Upload profile picture (optional)
   - Add emergency contact information
   - Link your children to your account

### Password Security
- Use a strong password (8+ characters)
- Include numbers and letters
- Change password regularly
- Never share your password

## Managing Your Children

### Adding Children

If your child isn't linked to your account:

1. **Contact Administrator**
   - Call your nursery office
   - Provide child's full name and birth date
   - Administrator will link the child to your account

2. **Verify Information**
   - Check child's profile is correct
   - Update any missing information
   - Upload child's photo (optional)

#### Educational Information

## Daily Operations

### Attendance Tracking

#### Morning Drop-off
1. **Arrive at Nursery**
   - Bring child to designated drop-off area
   - Staff will scan QR code or check child in manually

2. **Check-in Confirmation**
   - Receive SMS notification when child is checked in
   - View check-in time in the app
   - See staff member who checked child in

#### Afternoon Pick-up
1. **Authorized Pick-up**
   - Only authorized persons can pick up children
   - Show ID if requested by staff
   - Sign out electronically or manually

2. **Check-out Confirmation**
   - Receive SMS when child is checked out
   - View check-out time and person who picked up

### Daily Reports

#### Viewing Reports
1. **Access Daily Reports**
   - Go to "Daily Reports" in the menu
   - Select your child
   - Choose the date

2. **Report Contents**
   - **Activities**: What your child did today
   - **Meals**: Breakfast, lunch, snacks consumed
   - **Nap Time**: Sleep duration and quality
   - **Mood**: How your child was feeling
   - **Learning**: Educational activities and progress
   - **Health Notes**: Any health concerns or medications given
   - **Photos**: Pictures of activities (if available)

#### Report Features
- **Real-time Updates**: Reports updated throughout the day
- **Photo Gallery**: View activity photos
- **Comments**: Add notes or questions for teachers
- **Print/Save**: Download reports for records

## Communication Tools

### Notifications

#### Types of Notifications
- **Daily Reports**: New activity reports available
- **Attendance**: Check-in/check-out confirmations
- **Announcements**: Important nursery news
- **Appointments**: Upcoming meetings or events
- **Reminders**: Medication, special events, etc.

#### Managing Notifications
- **Settings**: Choose notification preferences
- **SMS**: Receive text messages
- **App Notifications**: In-app alerts
- **Email**: Email notifications (if configured)

### Messaging Staff

#### Sending Messages
1. **Access Messages**
   - Go to "Notifications" → "Messages"
   - Select recipient (teacher, manager, etc.)

2. **Message Types**
   - **General Questions**: About daily activities
   - **Health Updates**: Medical information
   - **Schedule Changes**: Pick-up time changes
   - **Concerns**: Report issues or feedback

#### Response Time
- Emergency messages: Immediate response
- General questions: Within 24 hours
- Non-urgent: Within 48 hours

### Appointments

#### Scheduling Appointments
1. **Request Meeting**
   - Go to "Appointments" → "Schedule New"
   - Choose date and time
   - Select purpose (parent-teacher conference, etc.)

2. **Appointment Types**
   - **Parent-Teacher Conferences**: Discuss child's progress
   - **Manager Meetings**: Address concerns or questions
   - **Special Events**: Open houses, performances
   - **Medical Discussions**: Health-related meetings

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### Can't Login
- **Check Phone Number**: Ensure correct format (+962...)
- **OTP Issues**: Wait 5 minutes for new code
- **Password Reset**: Contact administrator
- **App Issues**: Clear cache, try different browser

#### Reports Not Loading
- **Internet Connection**: Check WiFi/data
- **Browser Issues**: Try incognito mode
- **App Updates**: Update to latest version
- **Contact Support**: If problem persists

#### Notifications Not Received
- **Check Settings**: Verify notification preferences
- **Phone Settings**: Check Do Not Disturb mode
- **SMS Settings**: Ensure SMS is enabled
- **Contact Support**: For delivery issues

#### Photos Not Showing
- **Internet Connection**: Required for photo loading
- **Browser Settings**: Enable images
- **App Permissions**: Grant photo access
- **Contact Staff**: For photo upload issues
